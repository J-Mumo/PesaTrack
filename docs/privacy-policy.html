🔒 PesaTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Key point: Your transactions, SMS messages, amounts, and personal information stay on your device. The only data that ever leaves your phone is anonymous usage analytics — and only if you explicitly opt in. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy.

1. What PesaTrack Does

PesaTrack is a passive expense tracker for Android. It reads both incoming and outgoing M-PESA and bank SMS messages, parses transaction details (amount, recipient, date, transaction code), and saves them locally on your device for categorization, budgeting, and review.

2. SMS Data We Access

PesaTrack requests the following Android permissions:

  • RECEIVE_SMS — To detect M-PESA and supported bank SMS messages (incoming and outgoing transactions) in real time
  • READ_SMS — To import historical transaction SMS from your message inbox (user-initiated only)

PesaTrack only reads SMS from specific senders:

  • M-PESA (sender: "MPESA")
  • NCBA Bank (sender: "NCBA")

Personal SMS messages from contacts, promotional messages, and SMS from unsupported senders are never read, accessed, or stored.

3. What Data Is Stored On Your Device

From each qualifying transaction SMS, PesaTrack extracts and stores locally:

  • Transaction amount (KES)
  • Recipient name and number/till/paybill
  • Transaction date and time
  • M-PESA transaction code (e.g., "ABC123XYZ")
  • Transaction cost (if present in the SMS)
  • Payment type (Send Money, Buy Goods, Pay Bill, etc.)

Additionally, you may manually enter expenses or import from Excel spreadsheets. Category assignments, budgets, and settings are also stored locally in a Room database and DataStore preferences on your device.

4. Anonymous Usage Analytics (Opt-In)

Starting in version 1.5.0, PesaTrack can send anonymous usage analytics to help us understand which features are helpful and which need work. This is off by default. On first launch (or after updating from an older version) you will see a one-time prompt asking whether you'd like to help — you can say yes, dismiss it, or change your mind later.

What we send when analytics are on:

  • Which screens you open (e.g., "home", "analytics", "budget")
  • Which features you tap (e.g., "import completed", "budget created")
  • App version, Android version, device model, and country
  • Crash and error signals
  • An anonymous, rotating installation ID generated by Firebase Analytics

What we never send, even with analytics on:

  • The content of any SMS message
  • Transaction amounts, merchants, till/paybill numbers, or transaction codes
  • Category names, budget names, or note text you type in the app
  • Your name, phone number, email address, or M-PESA number
  • Anything else that could identify you personally

Analytics are processed by Google Firebase Analytics. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy for how they handle this data. We do not use Firebase for advertising, personalization, or any purpose beyond product improvement.

Turning analytics off: Open PesaTrack, tap the ⚙ Settings icon, and toggle "Anonymous usage data" off. When you do this, we immediately stop collection and reset the anonymous installation ID so the previous session's identifier cannot be associated with future data.

5. Data Sharing

Your transaction data is never shared. The only data that leaves your device is the anonymous analytics described in Section 4 — and only if you opt in.

  • Transaction data is never sent to any server or cloud service
  • Transaction data is never shared with any third party, advertising network, or SDK
  • No data — transactional or analytical — is sold, rented, or licensed to any party

5.5 User-Initiated Sharing

Certain features allow you to voluntarily share information outside the app:

  • Feedback emails: When you tap "Contact & Feedback," a draft email is prepared. Usage context (e.g., app version, usage milestones) may be included to help us assist you. You can review and remove any information before sending.
  • Backup files: Database backups that you create and export may contain your expense data and settings. These files stay on your device unless you choose to share them.
  • CSV exports: If you export your expenses to a CSV file, the file stays on your device unless you share it via Android's share sheet.

In all cases, you initiate the sharing action and can review exactly what is being sent before it leaves your device.

5.6 Google Play Services

PesaTrack integrates the following Google services:

  • Firebase Analytics — for the opt-in analytics described in Section 4
  • Google Play In-App Review API — may prompt you to rate the app within the Google Play review flow. No personal data is shared with PesaTrack through this process.

6. Data Security

PesaTrack offers an optional PIN lock with biometric unlock (fingerprint/face) to prevent unauthorized access to the app. The PIN is stored as a salted SHA-256 hash — the raw PIN is never stored.

Your data inherits the security of your Android device (device encryption, screen lock). PesaTrack does not implement additional database encryption, as the app's threat model is casual-access prevention, not forensic-level protection.

Analytics data (when enabled) is transmitted to Firebase over HTTPS/TLS.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • View: All your expense data is visible in the app at all times
  • Edit: You can re-categorize, exclude, or modify any expense
  • Delete on-device data: Clear all app data via Android Settings → Apps → PesaTrack → Clear Data
  • Disable analytics: Toggle off in PesaTrack → Settings → Privacy at any time
  • Reset analytics ID: Turning analytics off also resets the anonymous Firebase installation ID
  • Uninstall: Removing the app deletes all stored data permanently

Because analytics data is anonymous and not tied to your identity, we cannot look up or delete specific analytics records on request. Turning analytics off stops future collection and severs the link between past and future sessions on your device.

8. Children's Privacy

PesaTrack is not directed at children under 13. The app requires access to financial SMS messages, which is inherently an adult use case.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the updated version will be posted at this URL and the "Last updated" date will be revised. Material changes affecting data handling will be communicated through an app update.
