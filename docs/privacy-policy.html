🔒 PesaTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Key point: Individual transaction records and SMS stay on your device. Optional feature data leaves only when you choose to use or enable that feature: anonymous analytics (§4), subscription verification (§5.7), an anonymized spending digest for Coach Insights (§5.8), or your question and recent chat context for Ask Your Money (§5.9). The chat transcript remains local until you clear it or remove app data. No raw SMS, individual transaction records, or real merchant names are sent.

1. What PesaTrack Does

PesaTrack is a passive expense tracker for Android. It reads both incoming and outgoing M-PESA and bank SMS messages, parses transaction details (amount, recipient, date, transaction code), and saves them locally on your device for categorization, budgeting, and review.

2. SMS Data We Access

PesaTrack requests the following Android permissions:

  • RECEIVE_SMS — To detect M-PESA and supported bank SMS messages (incoming and outgoing transactions) in real time
  • READ_SMS — To import historical transaction SMS from your message inbox (user-initiated only)

PesaTrack only reads SMS from specific senders:

  • M-PESA (sender: "MPESA")
  • NCBA Bank (sender: "NCBA")

Personal SMS messages from contacts, promotional messages, and SMS from unsupported senders are never read, accessed, or stored.

3. What Data Is Stored On Your Device

From each qualifying transaction SMS, PesaTrack extracts and stores locally:

  • Transaction amount (KES)
  • Recipient name and number/till/paybill
  • Transaction date and time
  • M-PESA transaction code (e.g., "ABC123XYZ")
  • Transaction cost (if present in the SMS)
  • Payment type (Send Money, Buy Goods, Pay Bill, etc.)

Additionally, you may manually enter expenses or import from Excel spreadsheets. Category assignments, budgets, and settings are also stored locally in a Room database and DataStore preferences on your device.

Ask Your Money messages are also stored in the local Room database so you can resume the conversation after closing or restarting the app. They stay on this device until you choose Clear chat, clear app data, or uninstall PesaTrack. The transcript is not synced to a PesaTrack account or stored as chat history on our backend.

4. Anonymous Usage Analytics (Opt-In)

Starting in version 1.5.0, PesaTrack can send anonymous usage analytics to help us understand which features are helpful and which need work. This is off by default. On first launch (or after updating from an older version) you will see a one-time prompt asking whether you'd like to help — you can say yes, dismiss it, or change your mind later.

What we send when analytics are on:

  • Which screens you open (e.g., "home", "analytics", "budget")
  • Which features you tap (e.g., "import completed", "budget created")
  • App version, Android version, device model, and country
  • Crash and error signals
  • An anonymous, rotating installation ID generated by Firebase Analytics

What we never send, even with analytics on:

  • The content of any SMS message
  • Transaction amounts, merchants, till/paybill numbers, or transaction codes
  • Category names, budget names, or note text you type in the app
  • Your name, phone number, email address, or M-PESA number
  • Anything else that could identify you personally

Analytics are processed by Google Firebase Analytics. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy for how they handle this data. We do not use Firebase for advertising, personalization, or any purpose beyond product improvement.

Turning analytics off: Open PesaTrack, tap the ⚙ Settings icon, and toggle "Anonymous usage data" off. When you do this, we immediately stop collection and reset the anonymous installation ID so the previous session's identifier cannot be associated with future data.

5. Data Sharing

Individual transaction records are never shared. Optional features send limited data as described below; no raw SMS or individual transaction row is sent.

  • Individual transaction records and raw SMS are never sent to a server or cloud service
  • Transaction records are never shared with ad networks or analytics SDKs
  • No data — transactional or analytical — is sold, rented, or licensed to any party

5.5 User-Initiated Sharing

Certain features allow you to voluntarily share information outside the app:

  • Feedback emails: When you tap "Contact & Feedback," a draft email is prepared. Usage context (e.g., app version, usage milestones) may be included to help us assist you. You can review and remove any information before sending.
  • Backup files: Database backups that you create and export may contain your expense data, settings, and saved Ask Your Money chat transcript. These files stay on your device unless you choose to share them.
  • CSV exports: If you export your expenses to a CSV file, the file stays on your device unless you share it via Android's share sheet.

In all cases, you initiate the sharing action and can review exactly what is being sent before it leaves your device.

5.6 Google Play Services

PesaTrack integrates the following Google services:

  • Firebase Analytics — for the opt-in analytics described in Section 4
  • Google Play In-App Review API — may prompt you to rate the app within the Google Play review flow. No personal data is shared with PesaTrack through this process.

5.7 Subscription Billing (PesaTrack Pro, from v1.6.0)

Starting in version 1.6.0 you can optionally subscribe to PesaTrack Pro. The subscription itself runs through Google Play; PesaTrack also talks to a small backend of ours at pesatrack-api.jmumo.com so we can verify the purchase and check that it's still active. This section describes exactly what leaves your device if you subscribe. If you never open the Pro screen or complete a purchase, nothing in this section happens.

What we send when you subscribe:

  • Your Google Play purchase token — a token Google Play generates for the specific purchase. We send it once to POST /billing/verify so the backend can confirm the purchase against Google's Play Developer API.
  • The subscription product ID you selected (e.g. pesatrack_pro_monthly).

What the backend stores about you:

  • A SHA-256 hash of the purchase token — never the raw token itself. That hash is the entire identity anchor: there is no user account, no username, no email, no phone, no device ID stored server-side. The hash is used to look up your entitlement on later requests via the Authorization: Bearer header.
  • The normalized entitlement metadata Google Play returns for your purchase — product ID, expiry timestamp, auto-renewing flag, and payment state. These fields let us tell your app whether the subscription is still live without round-tripping to Google on every AI call.
  • A bounded audit trail (event type, hashed token, and a small metadata JSON with product ID, auto-renewing flag, payment state) so we can debug billing bugs. No request bodies, no free-form text, never anything that identifies you personally.

What the backend never stores:

  • The raw purchase token.
  • Your Google account email, name, or profile.
  • Anything from your transaction data — SMS content, amounts, recipient names, transaction IDs, category labels — none of it is sent to pesatrack-api.jmumo.com at any point.

Transport and server:

  • All traffic to pesatrack-api.jmumo.com is HTTPS/TLS only. The app's network security config pins that host to system trust anchors and disallows cleartext, in both release and debug builds.
  • Server logs record only the first 16 hex chars of the token hash for triage — never the full hash, never the raw token, never the request body.
  • The server runs on our own Hetzner Cloud VM (EU region) behind Caddy. Backups of the entitlement database are stored on the same host and never leave it.

Payments and refunds are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment method or your Google account. Cancel or request a refund the same way you would for any Play Store subscription; your entitlement lasts until the end of the paid period.

5.8 Coach Insights (PesaTrack Pro, from v1.7.0)

Starting in version 1.7.0, an active PesaTrack Pro subscription unlocks a daily Coach Insight card on the Home screen — one AI-generated observation about your spending pattern, with specific numbers and one thing you could try. Producing that card requires PesaTrack to send an anonymized spending digest to our backend at pesatrack-api.jmumo.com, which in turn calls OpenAI (our AI provider) with the same digest. This section describes exactly what leaves your device if you are subscribed and the AI feature is enabled for your build. If you are not subscribed, or the AI feature is off, nothing in this section happens.

What we send when a Coach Insight is generated:

  • A period label (e.g. "2026-09") and how far into it you are (day 15 of 30).
  • Aggregated category totals for the current period, the previous complete period, and a three-month rolling average. Amounts are whole KES only. Category names are the app's own display labels (e.g. "Food & Dining", "Transport") — never personalized.
  • Opaque recipient IDs for your top few recipients — the strings r1, r2, … through r8 — with each id's total spend, transaction count, and best-guess category. IDs are assigned locally on your device per request, so the same merchant can receive different ids on different days, and there is no way for the backend to correlate a merchant across users, sessions, or requests.
  • Detected recurring expense summaries (label, average amount, cycle, confidence) and current-period totals for income and money moved into savings.

What we never send:

  • The raw SMS body of any transaction.
  • Any individual transaction record.
  • Real merchant / recipient names. Names live only on your device; the id-to-name map is held in memory for the duration of the single AI request and is never persisted, never transmitted. Name substitution happens after the AI response returns, on device.
  • Your phone number, name, email, contacts, device ID, or Google account details.
  • Location data of any kind.

Server-side handling:

  • The backend at pesatrack-api.jmumo.com verifies your entitlement (the same SHA-256-of-purchase-token identity anchor described in §5.7), forwards the digest to OpenAI, applies additional content guardrails (the response is rejected if it recommends specific securities, brokers, or claims guaranteed returns), and caches successful responses by a hash of the digest for up to 24 hours so two requests with identical digests share one AI call.
  • The backend logs metadata about each call — request id, token-hash prefix, model id, token counts, response category. It never logs the digest content or the AI response text.
  • OpenAI receives the digest as described above (opaque ids, aggregated numbers, category labels) via API. Under OpenAI's API terms, that input is not used to train future models.

AI feature usage: Requests to POST /ai/coach-insight are rate-limited per entitlement to three per day and one per minute — so at most three Coach Insights are generated for you in any 24-hour window. The client caches the most recent successful insight locally for one day; when a fetch fails for any reason, yesterday's cached insight or the free-tier template is shown silently — no error message, no "AI unavailable" text.

Cancelling or disabling AI features: Cancel the PesaTrack Pro subscription in Google Play to stop all AI feature usage from your device. Existing cached insights on-device are cleared when the subscription entitlement lapses.

5.9 Ask Your Money Chat (PesaTrack Pro)

When you choose to ask a question, the app sends your question, up to the last 10 finalized chat turns, and the anonymized spending digest described in §5.8 to our backend. The backend forwards that request to OpenAI to generate the answer. The digest contains aggregated totals and opaque recipient IDs, not raw SMS or individual transactions. Do not include passwords, account credentials, or other sensitive information in a chat message.

The transcript is saved locally so you can resume it. Our backend does not save the question, prior turns, or generated answer as a conversation history; OpenAI processes the prompt under its API terms. You can erase the on-device transcript at any time with Clear chat. Clearing it does not recall a request already sent to generate an earlier answer.

6. Data Security

PesaTrack offers an optional PIN lock with biometric unlock (fingerprint/face) to prevent unauthorized access to the app. The PIN is stored as a salted SHA-256 hash — the raw PIN is never stored.

Your data inherits the security of your Android device (device encryption, screen lock). PesaTrack does not implement additional database encryption, as the app's threat model is casual-access prevention, not forensic-level protection.

Analytics data (when enabled) is transmitted to Firebase over HTTPS/TLS.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • View: All your expense data is visible in the app at all times
  • Edit: You can re-categorize, exclude, or modify any expense
  • Delete on-device data: Clear all app data via Android Settings → Apps → PesaTrack → Clear Data
  • Disable analytics: Toggle off in PesaTrack → Settings → Privacy at any time
  • Reset analytics ID: Turning analytics off also resets the anonymous Firebase installation ID
  • Uninstall: Removing the app deletes all stored data permanently

Because analytics data is anonymous and not tied to your identity, we cannot look up or delete specific analytics records on request. Turning analytics off stops future collection and severs the link between past and future sessions on your device.

8. Children's Privacy

PesaTrack is not directed at children under 13. The app requires access to financial SMS messages, which is inherently an adult use case.
