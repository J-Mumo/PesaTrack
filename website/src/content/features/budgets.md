  - Let you set a monthly budget per category (Transport, Groceries, Airtime, etc.)

  - Send a soft notification when a category is nearing or over budget (respects the "one insight per session" rule)
  - Include income allocation (headroom = income − committed spend) when income is set
doesNotDo:
  - Show projections or forecasts of what you "will" spend (removed in v1.3.2 — see /changelog)
  - Use fear-based copy ("you're overspending") or streaks
  - Roll over surpluses in a way that hides real spend

## The philosophy

Budgets are a decision tiebreaker, not a leash. PesaTrack shows the
neutral fact — *"KES 3,400 remaining for Groceries, 12 days left"* — and
lets you decide what to do. There is no gamified streak, no red "over"
badge, no push notification designed to make you feel bad.

If you don't set a budget for a category, PesaTrack doesn't invent one.
