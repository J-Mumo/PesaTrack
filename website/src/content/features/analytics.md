## Investment illustration — what the math actually says

The Monthly Review's "What your savings could become" card takes the amount
you actually invested (or your headroom, if you didn't) and shows what a
**single deposit at 10% annual return, compounded monthly, over 5 years**
would grow to. The assumption line is always visible — no hidden rate, no
implied "per month" contribution.

If you didn't save anything that month, the illustration doesn't show. We
don't pretend numbers exist that don't.
