## Install from Google Play

PesaTrack is a free Android app.

1. Open the Google Play Store on your phone.
2. Search for **PesaTrack** or open the [Play Store listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pesatrack) directly.
3. Tap **Install**. The download is small (under 20 MB).
4. Once installed, tap **Open**.

## First launch

On the first launch you will see:

1. **Welcome screen.** A short summary of what the app does. Tap **Get started**.
2. **SMS permission prompt.** Android asks whether PesaTrack can read incoming
   SMS. Grant this to enable automatic transaction detection. Without it, the
   app still works for manual entry and imports — but the core "passive tracking"
   feature will not run. See [Granting SMS permission](/docs/granting-sms-permission).
3. **Onboarding.** A short tour of the Home, Analytics, and Budgets screens.
4. **Analytics opt-in.** An optional one-time prompt for anonymous usage
   analytics. It is off by default; skip it and the app works the same.
   See the [Privacy policy](/privacy) for what analytics do and don't send.

## What to do next

- Let the app receive one or two M-PESA SMS. Open **Home** — the new expenses
  will appear categorised.
- Set your budget cycle: **Settings → Month starts on**. See
  [Setting month starts on](/docs/month-starts-on).
- If you have older M-PESA transactions in an inbox, use **Settings → Import
  from SMS** to backfill them in one pass.

## System requirements

- Android 8.0 (Oreo) or newer.
- Enough free storage for the app database — a year of typical usage is under
  10 MB.

## Something went wrong?

If the install fails or the app won't open, contact
[support](/support) with your Android version and phone model.
