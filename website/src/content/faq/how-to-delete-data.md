---
question: How do I delete my data?
group: privacy-data
order: 30
anchor: how-to-delete-data
---

Two ways. **Uninstall** removes everything — Android deletes the app's database and
preferences with it. Or **Android Settings → Apps → PesaTrack → Storage → Clear data**
if you want to wipe without uninstalling. There's no server to contact, no account to
close.
