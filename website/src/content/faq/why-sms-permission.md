---
question: Why does PesaTrack need SMS permission?
group: sms-permissions
order: 10
anchor: why-sms-permission
---

To detect M-PESA and NCBA transaction SMS the moment they arrive and parse the amount,
recipient, and transaction code into an expense — automatically, without you retyping
anything. Without the permission the app still works, but you have to enter expenses
manually or import them from a PDF statement.
