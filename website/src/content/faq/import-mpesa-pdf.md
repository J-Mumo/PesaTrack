---
question: How do I import an M-PESA PDF statement?
group: imports
order: 10
anchor: import-mpesa-pdf
---

Open the M-PESA app → Statement → email yourself the PDF. Open the PDF on your phone,
tap Share, pick PesaTrack. The parser reads the statement locally and inserts new rows,
skipping any transaction ID it's seen before. Nothing about the file leaves your device.
