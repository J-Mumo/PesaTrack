---
question: Can I use PesaTrack without granting SMS permission?
group: sms-permissions
order: 40
anchor: without-sms-permission
---

Yes. Skip the permission prompt during onboarding (or deny it later in Android Settings)
and you can add expenses manually or import from a PDF/Excel file. You lose real-time
detection but the rest of the app — categories, budgets, analytics, backups — works
identically.
