---
question: Does PesaTrack use any analytics or trackers?
group: privacy-data
order: 20
anchor: analytics-trackers
---

The only telemetry is **opt-in anonymous Firebase Analytics** — off by default, gated
behind a one-time prompt, and toggleable at any time in Settings → Privacy. When it's on
we receive screen-open and feature-use events plus your app version, Android version,
device model, and country. When it's off, nothing goes out.

No ad SDKs. No third-party trackers. No cross-app identifiers.
